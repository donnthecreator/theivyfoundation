What The Process is

Short lessons on a tablet, group sessions, and monthly visits from an Ivy Foundation mentor. Lessons cover handling pressure, school plans, getting ready for work, money, and the first month home. Taking part is voluntary.

What we collect

  • Your child's name, where they're enrolled, and a 4-digit PIN they chose
  • Their answers to a short getting-started survey (things like age, school, interests, and what could get in the way, like rides)
  • Their lesson progress and the plan they write
  • Notes from mentor visits
  • Your name, relationship, and email

Who can see it

Ivy Foundation program staff and your child's assigned mentor. We do not sell this information.

What we won't do without asking you

We won't share your child's information with employers, partner businesses, or anyone outside the program, except where the law requires it, such as a concern for someone's safety.

Your choices

  • You can ask to see what we have about your child.
  • You can withdraw permission at any time and ask us to delete your child's records.
